Awaaz

One call screen, every way of talking.

Switch how you talk at any moment: type and Awaaz speaks, use your own voice, or say a few words and let AI phrase it. Replies arrive as captions, clear audio, or both.

Try each way of talking

  • Type. Tap a suggested reply or type; the pharmacist hears it in your voice.
  • My voice. For deaf people who speak: tap “Hold to talk” and talk normally. You still read their replies as captions.
  • Help me say. Type a few words like “delivery?” and AI drafts a polite sentence. Nothing is said until you tap Say it.

Also in this screen

  • Number chips for prices and times, tap to save.
  • Please hold. Pause typing for 5 seconds and Awaaz tells them you’re typing.
  • Ask again politely asks them to repeat.
  • Scam shield blocks reading out OTPs.

Set how you talk and how you hear.

Every person is different. Pick a starting point, then turn on any mix of options. Each contact can override it, and you can switch mid-call.

For people who can speak, but can’t hear well.

You talk normally. After each sentence, Awaaz says it again in your ear, slower and louder, in your language. No reading needed: three buttons, or just say “phir se”.

How it works

  • Your phone rings from Awaaz, or you call through the app with headphones or hearing aids.
  • Exact words. Awaaz repeats what they said, only translated if you asked. Numbers, dates and names never change.
  • They hear a soft tone during the repeat, so they pause.
  • Voice commands work in Hindi, Marathi and English: “phir se”, “dheere”, “number kya tha”.

Hand the whole call to Awaaz.

For anyone who can’t or doesn’t want to do the call live. Pick a task, set limits, approve anything outside them, and get the result back.

What the agent may and may not do

  • Opens honestly: “I’m an assistant calling on behalf of Shankar More, who is deaf.”
  • Shares only the facts on the task: consumer number, address, medicine name.
  • Asks you first before agreeing to a price, date or change outside your limits.
  • Never shares OTPs, PINs or card numbers, and never agrees to pay.

Start it from anywhere

  • The app, a WhatsApp message to the Awaaz bot, or a voice note.
  • Results come back in the app and on WhatsApp.

Awaaz inside WhatsApp.

No app needed. Chat with the Awaaz bot to start calls and tasks, read live captions as messages, turn voice notes into text, and send voice notes in your own voice.

Use Awaaz on calls to your own number.

Apps can’t listen to normal phone calls, so Awaaz joins as a third person. On Android, when Awaaz is your phone app, it adds itself with one tap. The other person still sees your own number.

At the counter, the clinic, or a speakerphone call.

Lay the phone between you. The top half faces the other person and shows what you typed or said; the bottom half shows you their words as captions. Also works next to a speakerphone, for WhatsApp or video calls on another device.

When to use it

  • In person: pharmacy counter, doctor’s room, bank desk, auto-rickshaw.
  • WhatsApp or video calls on a laptop or second phone: put that call on speaker, keep Awaaz open nearby.
  • Group conversations: captions show who is speaking.
