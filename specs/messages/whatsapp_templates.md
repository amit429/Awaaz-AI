# WhatsApp templates (submit on day 1; ⚠️ approval can take days)

Outside the 24-hour service window only approved templates can be sent. Needs native-speaker review.

1. `welcome_v1` — “Namaste {{1}}! Main Awaaz hoon. Mujhe likhiye ya voice note bhejiye: main aapke liye call kar sakta hoon, voice notes padh kar suna sakta hoon, aur aapki awaaz mein jawab bhej sakta hoon.” / English: “Hi {{1}}, I’m Awaaz. Message me or send a voice note: I can make calls for you, turn voice notes into text, and send replies in your own voice.”
2. `approval_v1` — “{{1}} asks: {{2}}” with buttons **Approve** / **Decline**.
3. `task_result_v1` — “{{1}} done. {{2}}. Reference: {{3}}.”
4. `call_summary_v1` — “Call with {{1}}: {{2}}”
5. `family_share_v1` — “{{1}} shared a call summary with you: {{2}}”

Session messages (inside the window) are free-form: live captions (`🟢 {{contact}}: {{caption}}`), transcripts, voice notes, quick-reply buttons.
